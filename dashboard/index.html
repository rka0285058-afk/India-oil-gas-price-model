How it works: a regime-switching Brent model (calm, simmering disruption, acute escalation, glut) feeds the rupee, the Indian crude basket, LNG and domestic gas formulas, a pump-price rule that mimics excise cuts, oil-marketing-company losses and price hikes, an administered LPG price that follows the Saudi contract price with a lag, and a CNG price that follows the domestic-plus-LNG gas basket. Each scenario changes the odds of moving between regimes and the Russian-crude discount.

Calibrated to PPAC, RBI, EIA, IEA and OPEC figures; freight, VAT, refining margins, LNG contract mix, the LPG cost residual, the CNG gas-basket mix and scenario weights are assumptions. LPG and CNG are Delhi prices (14.2 kg domestic cylinder, IGL CNG). Not investment advice.
